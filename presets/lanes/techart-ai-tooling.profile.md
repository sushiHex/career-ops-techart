## Your Target Roles

A **dual-track** search: technical art (primary) plus AI/ML tooling (secondary) and
applied AI (adjacent). Classify every offer into one of these archetypes, or a hybrid of
two. The first four are technical-art flavours; the last two are the engineering track.

| Archetype | Track | Key signals in JD |
|-----------|-------|-------------------|
| Technical Artist (generalist / Senior-Lead-Principal) | tech-art | "technical artist", "TA", "DCC", "art pipeline", "tooling", "real-time", "Unreal", "Unity", "shader", "bridge art and engineering" |
| Pipeline TA | tech-art | "pipeline", "asset pipeline", "USD / OpenUSD", "automation", "Python", "PyMEL", "MEL", "tools engineer", "build/validation", "DCC integration", "Perforce" |

Record the **track** (tech-art or ai-ml) with the archetype. It selects the comp range:
the top-level `compensation` block in `config/profile.yml` for tech-art, and a matching
`alternate_ranges` entry for ai-ml if you define one.

## Portfolio Evidence

For every tech-art archetype, the demo reel, ArtStation, IMDb credits and shipped titles
are first-class evidence. Weight them inside **Match with CV** and **North Star** alongside
written bullets: a role that lets the reel do the talking (character, look-dev, real-time
cinematic) fits better than one where visual craft is irrelevant. For the ai-ml track,
weight shipped tooling and automation impact instead; the reel is secondary there. For a
tech-art role the reel IS the case study, so Block F recommends a reel walkthrough: which
shots to present and in what order.

## Comp Sources

For tech-art, games, film and XR roles prefer games-industry sources: Levels.fyi by studio
and level, Glassdoor, Hitmarker, the GDC salary survey, Built In, and the Animation Guild
(TAG Local 839) rate card where union studios apply. Studios often weight base, bonus and
relocation differently from tech. For AI/ML tooling roles use Levels.fyi, Glassdoor and
Blind as usual.

## Red-flag Questions

Prepare answers for the objections a dual-track background draws:
- **"Why a TA role when you also do AI tooling?" / "Why not pick one?"** The combination is
  the value: a TA who can build the AI-assisted tooling the art team actually uses.
- Add the questions your own history invites (a long tenure, a gap, a layoff) with the
  answer you have decided on. Keep each brief, factual and forward-looking.
